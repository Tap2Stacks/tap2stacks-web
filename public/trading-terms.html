tap2stacks Trading Terms

Trading eligibility

US persons may not trade on tap2stacks. You also may not trade while located in the United States, regardless of whether you are a US person.

You must be at least 18 years old to trade. Before trading, you must confirm that you are at least 18, are not a US person, and are not located in the United States.

Testnet only

tap2stacks is a Base Sepolia testnet game. Test USDC has no monetary value.
